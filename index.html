Pay with Sepolia ETH via MetaMask (testnet only)

Connect MetaMask, then send Sepolia ETH — test ETH with no real value, never mainnet ETH. You type the recipient and the amount; the site never invents a recipient. A safe first test is 0.001 Sepolia ETH to your own address, which is filled in automatically after you connect.

Before sending, the site switches the wallet to Sepolia and verifies eth_chainId is 0xaa36a7. If the wallet is not on Sepolia after switching, the payment is aborted.

Claim your finished tale on Sepolia (optional)

After a tale finishes, you can record it as a real Sepolia transaction: 0 ETH to yourself, with the tale result encoded in the calldata. No contract, no custody, nothing is collected. Gas is test ETH only.

The claim checks that the wallet is on Sepolia (chain 11155111, 0xaa36a7) before sending, and pins that chain ID on the transaction.

Data: api.squeekchain.com — /v1/events?category=protocol (system.settlement_completed) and /v1/status. Round classes: 0 work = nap, 1–499 = jog, 500+ = zoomies. Your tale is saved in this browser only.
