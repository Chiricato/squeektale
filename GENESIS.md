## Display name

SqueekTale

## How does this project use Squeekchain? (one short answer)

A choice-driven living story where each real SqueekCore settlement batch
writes the next chapter: the batch's work decides whether the chapter
is a nap, a jog or zoomies, the player's action decides the outcome,
and MetaMask can pay Sepolia ETH (testnet) or claim a finished tale as
a verifiable Sepolia transaction.

## Verification — direct route (from the Genesis FAQ)

1. Add the DNS record Genesis gives you to your own domain.
2. Add the page tag Genesis gives you to the live site.
3. Add the repository line Genesis gives you to this README.
4. Press Verify.

Then the Squeekchain team reviews the project before it appears on
the board. If you instead build through IMD, pay for the project's
first job from a wallet verified on your Genesis profile (a regular
wallet, not a smart-contract one).

## Why this should pass the “finished build” check

- It is playable, not a landing page: pick an action, a chapter
  resolves, a tale ends with one of five endings.
- It uses Squeekchain for outcomes, not decoration: without a settled
  batch (or a labelled demo/recorded round) no chapter resolves.
- It is not a dashboard, tracker, betting app or copy: no existing
  Genesis project is a choice-driven story with these rules, and the
  code, text and balance table here are original to this project.
